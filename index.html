Blackthorn Evidence Lab

Five missions. Forty minutes. Build a case that can withstand a challenge.

Your assignment

You are an evidence-review panel. Your job is to distinguish evidence from inference, test witness limitations, and explain what remains unproven. There is no confirmed culprit in this packet.

Play individually or in pairs. For a shared screen, agree on an answer before your teacher selects it. Each mission has four challenges and a written reasoning task. Budget 8 minutes per mission: 2 to read, 3 to solve, 3 to discuss and write.

First answers are final: 5 points per correct answer, or 4 if you use its hint. Incorrect answers earn 0, but feedback helps you continue. Writing is saved for teacher review; word count checks completion, not quality.

Teacher launch guide and source notes

Today: November 18, 2026. Introduce the activity, then run this game for 40 minutes. If the whole class is only 45 minutes, allow 3 minutes to launch and 2 minutes to collect reports. Suggested checkpoints: finish missions 1–5 at 8, 16, 24, 32 and 40 minutes.

For pairs, assign a reader and a challenger; switch roles after each mission. At each checkpoint ask one pair to share a limitation they found. The timer is a pacing aid and never removes access to unfinished work.

Assessment: automated knowledge score is out of 100. Award each written response up to 4 separate teacher points: accurate claim, named source, reasoning, and a meaningful limitation or counterargument. Maximum written score: 20. Do not award marks for choosing a particular suspect.

Sharing: send this HTML file through your class platform, or upload it to a static website. Students open it in a browser and download their report to submit. This file has no live multiplayer, central results collection, or secure examination controls. No data is sent to a server.
